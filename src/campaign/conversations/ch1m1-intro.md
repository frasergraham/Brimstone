# Played before planning opens on round 1 of The Awakening.
# A roadside inn at dusk: Ishmael (hero) asks the way; the innkeeper warns him
# off the night road. A crash from the stable ends it and the fight begins.
#

hero: [cheerful] Good evening, sir. Is this the way to Caleb's Hollow?

innkeeper: You're on the right path indeed, stranger — [cautious] but the
  woods haven't been safe of late. I'd not ride them after dark.

hero: That's exactly why I'm here. My cousin wrote to me a month past,
  asking for my help. [scoffs] He called the town cursed — sounds to me
  like superstitious nonsense.

innkeeper: [sighs] Superstition can kill a man. Three hunters went into
  those woods last week. Only one came back, and he'll not speak a word of
  what he saw — just sits by the fire, staring.

hero: And my cousin? Thomas Webb — do you know him?

innkeeper: [slowly] Aye. I know Tom. Good man. Came through here, maybe a
  fortnight ago. Said he was going back to help the minister raise a militia.
  [sorrowful] That was the last I saw of him.

hero: [urgent] Then I've no time to waste. I'll continue on into the night.

innkeeper: You'll do no such thing. The road to Caleb's Hollow is a half-day's
  ride. Sleep here tonight and leave at dawn. The dead can wait.

# (A crash from the stable, round the corner. Both men turn.) — the beat that
# motivates the next line; a stable SFX/visual cue here is a TODO for staging.

innkeeper: [surprised] God's teeth — that came from the stable.

hero: Stay inside. I'll take a look.
