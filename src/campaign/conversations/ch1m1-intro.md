# Played before planning opens on round 1 of The Awakening.
# The innkeeper meets the hero outside the Inn, then hurries off north.

innkeeper: Thank the heavens — you're awake, ser. The dead are in the
  streets. They came with the mist, before first light.

hero: How many, John?

innkeeper: More than I could count through the shutters. And something
  bigger moving behind them, down by the church.

hero: Get inside. Bar the doors and keep the lamps low. I'll clear the
  village before nightfall.

innkeeper: God keep you, Paladin. I'll see to the folk on the north road.
