BRIMSTONE

A Salem Hex Game

A hero rides into a cursed New England town. The witch who haunts its graveyard stirs. One will prevail before the dawn.

Rules at a glance
  • Move — step to an adjacent hex
  • Explore — uncover loot, weapons, or survivors in buildings
  • Battle — attack an adjacent enemy
  • Fortify — spend 🪵 Wood (+1 DEF) or ⚙ Metal (+2 DEF) on a building
  • Equip — use a found weapon for permanent stat bonuses

🌅 Dawn/Dusk: balanced, no bonuses — transition phases.
☀ Day: Hero +1 ATK. Witch minions in the open take damage!
🌙 Night: Witch +1 ATK. Survivors in the open take damage!
🏰 Fortifications absorb hits before HP is lost — build them up!
⚔ Gang-up: +1 ATK or DEF when allies are nearby.
Witch wins by seizing 3 ⛧ Power Nodes, or killing the hero.

🕯 Witch — summon minions, seize the three Power Nodes before dawn, or slay the hero.
