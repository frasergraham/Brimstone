A Salem Hex Game

A hero rides into a cursed New England town. The witch who haunts its graveyard stirs. One will prevail before the dawn.

Rules at a glance
  • Move — step to an adjacent hex
  • Explore — uncover resources or survivors
  • Battle — attack an adjacent enemy
  • Fortify — spend Wood to defend a building (+1 defense)

☀ Day: Hero gets 4 actions, Witch gets 3.
🌙 Night: Witch gets 4, Hero gets 3.
Phase changes every 3 rounds.
