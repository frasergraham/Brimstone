BRIMSTONE

A hero rides into a cursed New England town. The witch who haunts its graveyard stirs. One will prevail before the dawn.

Admin

HOW TO PLAY

A turn-based hex strategy game set in cursed colonial Salem. Two asymmetric factions fight across a procedurally-generated map. Plans are submitted simultaneously each round, then resolved together.

Win Conditions

⚔ Hero wins by:
  • Slaying the Witch
  • Holding more Power Nodes at 4 scoring checkpoints
✦ Witch wins by:
  • Slaying the Hero
  • Seizing all 3 Power Nodes
  • Accumulating 4 node-score points

The Factions

⚔ Hero — 14 HP · ATK 3 · DEF 2 · 3 base actions
Explore buildings, recruit survivors, fortify positions. +1 action at Dawn & Day. Survivors grant extra actions.

✦ Witch — 10 HP · ATK 2 · DEF 2 · 4 base actions
Summon undead and golems, seize Power Nodes. +1 action at Night. Minions grant extra actions.

Phase Cycle (8 rounds per cycle)

🌅 DawnHero +1 action · attrition rises · Power Nodes scored
☀ DayHero +1 ATK · witch undead in the open take damage
🌇 DuskPower Nodes scored
🌙 NightWitch +1 ATK · survivors in the open take damage

Actions

MoveStep to an adjacent hex (2 hexes with a horse)
ExploreUncover loot, weapons, or survivors in buildings
BattleAttack an adjacent or co-located enemy
FortifyHero: spend 🪵 Wood (+1 DEF) or ⚙ Metal (+2 DEF), max 4
SummonWitch: spend resources to summon a minion or golem
Use ItemHerbs (free, +2 HP) · Food (+1 action) · Silver (+1 ATK) · Scripture (ward)
EquipFree — equip a found weapon for permanent stat bonuses

Combat

Both sides roll a d6 + stats. Hit if attack > defense (1 damage). Crush if attack ≥ 2× defense (2 damage). Counter if defense ≥ 2× attack (1 damage to attacker). Having an ally adjacent adds a bonus d3 die.

Power Nodes ⛧

Three cursed nodes appear on the map. At each Dawn and Dusk, whoever holds more nodes scores 1 point. First to 4 cumulative points wins. The Witch instantly wins by holding all 3 nodes at any scoring checkpoint.

Fog of War

Active when any AI is present. Hero sight range varies by phase: Day (3), Dawn/Dusk (2), Night (1). SCOUT survivors extend sight by 1. Enemies outside your sight are hidden.
