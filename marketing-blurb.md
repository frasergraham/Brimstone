# BRIMSTONE: Caleb's Hollow

## Tagline

*A cursed village. Two factions. One night to decide everything.*

---

## Short Blurb

The dead are rising in Caleb's Hollow. A witch conducts dark rituals from the shadows while a lone hero rallies desperate survivors to fight back. Plan your moves in secret, then watch chaos unfold as both sides act at once. No waiting for turns. No safe reactions. Just prediction, nerve, and the creeping dread of fog-shrouded New England darkness.

**Brimstone** is an asymmetric, simultaneous-turn strategy game on a procedurally generated hex grid. Command the Hero — exploring cursed buildings, recruiting named survivors, and fortifying against the night — or unleash the Witch, summoning undead minions and seizing Power Nodes to complete a dark ritual. The day/night cycle shifts the balance of power every round: daylight favors the living, but when night falls, the dead grow strong and sight shrinks to a single hex.

---

## Features

- **Simultaneous Planning** — Both sides build their plans in secret, then everything resolves at once. No downtime. No reactive play. Pure strategic prediction.

- **Asymmetric Factions** — The Hero explores, recruits, and fortifies. The Witch summons, corrupts, and overwhelms. Fundamentally different playstyles with multiple paths to victory.

- **Day/Night Cycle** — An 8-round cycle shifts power between factions. Dawn and dusk trigger scoring. Day empowers the Hero; night unleashes the Witch. Time your moves to the rhythm of the sky.

- **Multiple Victory Paths** — Slay the enemy leader. Seize all three Power Nodes. Outscore your opponent at dawn and dusk checkpoints. Every game ends differently.

- **Procedural Hex Maps** — Seeded generation creates unique battlefields of forests, rivers, bridges, roads, and colonial buildings — from quick Skirmish maps to sprawling Campaign landscapes.

- **Named Survivors & Unique Abilities** — Recruit 20+ survivors, each with a name, backstory, and special ability. The Blacksmith hits harder. The Nurse heals. The Scout sees further. Every rescue is a strategic choice.

- **Fog of War** — Enemies lurk beyond your sight range, which shrinks as darkness falls. You won't know where the Witch is striking until it's too late.

- **Six AI Personalities** — Face Berserker, Sentinel, Scavenger, Hoarder, and Swarm strategies. Each AI plays differently, keeping every match unpredictable.

- **Online Multiplayer** — 1 to 4 players per side. Team up with allies, coordinate node assaults, and outwit a human opponent who won't follow any script.

- **Story Campaign** — Six missions unravel the mystery of the curse, from the first zombie rising to a final confrontation in the dark forest.

## The Hook

Most strategy games let you react. Brimstone doesn't. You commit your plan, your opponent commits theirs, and then the world moves. Did you guess right? Did they? That moment — when both plans collide and the fog lifts — is what makes every round feel like a held breath finally released.

Gothic horror. Colonial dread. Tactical depth. No install, no dependencies — runs in your browser.
