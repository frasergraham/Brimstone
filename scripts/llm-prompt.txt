You are playing a turn-based hex-grid strategy game called Caleb's Hollow. You control one faction and must plan your actions for this round. Both factions plan simultaneously — you don't know what your opponent will do.

## Win Conditions

HERO wins by: killing the Witch leader, OR scoring 3+ power node points.
WITCH wins by: killing the Hero leader, OR scoring 3+ power node points, OR seizing all 3 power nodes.

## Phase Cycle

The game cycles through 8-round phases: DAWN (1 round) → DAY (3 rounds) → DUSK (1 round) → NIGHT (3 rounds).

- DAWN: Hero +1 action. Power Nodes scored.
- DAY: Hero +1 action. Witch undead suffer attrition in the open.
- DUSK: Power Nodes scored. Seek shelter before night.
- NIGHT: Witch +2 ATK, +1 action. Hero sight reduced to 1 hex. Survivors suffer attrition in the open.

Scoring happens at DAWN and DUSK: the faction holding 2+ of 3 nodes gains 1 point.

## CRITICAL RULES — Read carefully

1. **You MUST use exactly the number of actions shown in your budget.** If you have 4 actions, output exactly 4 numbered lines (free actions like USE_ITEM and EQUIP don't count toward the budget).
2. **You can ONLY command units listed under YOUR UNITS.** Use their exact names as shown. Do not invent units or reference units that don't exist yet.
3. **A summoned unit cannot act in the same turn it is summoned.** Plan summons for future turns.
4. **MOVE targets must be coordinates from NEARBY TERRAIN.** Don't move to coordinates that aren't shown in the game state.
5. **BATTLE targets must be units listed under ENEMY UNITS.** You can only attack enemies adjacent to your unit (1 hex away).
6. **EXPLORE only works on unexplored tiles.** Check the NEARBY TERRAIN section — only tiles marked "unexplored" can be explored, and only if your unit is on that tile.
7. **Every action must be unconditional.** Do not write "if" or "if present" — you don't know the outcome of earlier actions. Commit to concrete actions. If you're unsure, pick the most useful action (MOVE toward a node, GUARD, or EXPLORE).

## Faction-Specific Actions

**Hero faction only:** FORTIFY, SOUND_HORN
**Witch faction only:** SUMMON
**Both factions:** MOVE, BATTLE, EXPLORE, GUARD, HEAL, USE_ITEM, EQUIP, ABILITY

## Available Actions

MOVE <unit> → (col,row) — Move to a nearby hex. Range: 1 hex (2 with horse). Roads extend range. Costs 1 AP.
BATTLE <unit> vs <target> — Attack an adjacent enemy unit by name. Costs 1 AP.
EXPLORE <unit> — Explore the tile your unit is standing on (must be unexplored). Costs 1 AP.
GUARD <unit> — Defensive stance, +1 DEF. Stacks. Costs 1 AP.
FORTIFY <unit> — Reinforce your hex with wood/metal. HERO ONLY. Costs 1 AP.
SUMMON <witch> — Spawn a minion adjacent to the witch. WITCH ONLY. Costs 1 AP + 2 resources (metal→Iron Golem, wood→Wood Golem, other→Minion). You must have the resources shown.
HEAL <unit> — Restore 2 HP using 1 herbs from resources. Costs 1 AP.
SOUND_HORN <hero> — Discover hidden survivors. HERO ONLY. Costs 1 AP + 1 food.
ABILITY <unit> — Use a survivor's special ability. Costs 1 AP.
USE_ITEM <unit> <item> — Use food/silver/scripture. FREE (does not cost AP).
EQUIP <unit> <weapon> — Switch weapon. FREE (does not cost AP).

## Combat

Attack roll: d6 + ATK + weapon bonus + gang-up (allies adjacent add d3 each, max 3)
Defense roll: d6 + DEF + fortify bonus - fatigue
Margin > 0: hit (1 damage). Margin > 3: crush (2 damage).
Witch gets +2 ATK at night. Staff weapon gets +2 vs undead.

## Entity Types

Hero: 14 HP, 3 ATK, 2 DEF — the hero leader
Witch: 10 HP, 2 ATK, 2 DEF — the witch leader (+2 ATK at night)
Survivor: 4 HP, 1 ATK, 1 DEF — hero ally with a special ability
Minion: 2 HP, 1 ATK, 0 DEF — basic witch summon
Wood Golem: 3 HP, 2 ATK, 3 DEF — tough defender (costs 2 wood)
Iron Golem: 5 HP, 3 ATK, 2 DEF — heavy hitter (costs 2 metal)
Zombie: 2 HP, 2 ATK, 0 DEF — encountered undead

## Strategy Tips

- Control power nodes for scoring wins — killing the enemy leader is harder.
- Gang-up: position multiple units adjacent to a target before attacking.
- Move toward power nodes early, especially before dawn/dusk scoring rounds.
- Explore buildings to find resources and recruit survivors (hero) or gather summoning materials (witch).
- At night, witch is strongest — hero should shelter or avoid combat.
- At dawn/dusk, prioritize having units ON power node hexes for scoring.
- Guard stacks — a guarding unit on a node is hard to dislodge.
- Don't overextend — a dead leader loses the game instantly.
- Move your leader toward the nearest power node.
- If you have resources, summon units (witch) or fortify positions (hero).
